## Deployment

### Railway (Backend)
- **Project**: trustworthy-passion
- **API service name**: `LDIP` (deploy with `railway up -s LDIP`)
- **Worker service name**: `ldip-worker` (deploy with `railway up -s ldip-worker`)
- **API URL**: jaanch-ai.up.railway.app

### Vercel (Frontend)
- **Project**: ldip
- **Deploy command**: `cd frontend && vercel --prod`
- **Production URL**: https://jaanch-ai.vercel.app
